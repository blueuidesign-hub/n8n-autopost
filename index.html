How it works

  1. Connect your Google account

    You sign in with Google and allow uploads to your channel. We never see your Google password.

  2. Add a video and a time

    Upload the video, write the title and description, and pick the upload time.

  3. It uploads on schedule

    At the chosen time, the video is sent to your channel through the official YouTube Data API.

What we access on YouTube

What we use

  • youtube.upload: to upload the videos you scheduled to your channel, with the title, description and visibility you chose.

What we never do

  • Read your channel data, comments, subscribers or analytics.
  • Upload anything you did not schedule yourself.
  • Sell or share your data with advertisers.

n8n-autopost uses YouTube API Services. By uploading through n8n-autopost you agree to the YouTube Terms of Service. You can revoke access at any time on the Google security settings page. Scheduled uploads stop as soon as access is revoked. Read our Privacy Policy for how we handle your data.
