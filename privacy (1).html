Privacy Policy

Last updated: 28 September 2026

This policy explains what data n8n-autopost collects when you allow it to upload videos to your YouTube channel, how that data is used, and how you can have it deleted.

YouTube API Services

n8n-autopost uses YouTube API Services to upload the videos you schedule to the YouTube channel you authorize. By using n8n-autopost, you are also subject to the Google Privacy Policy.

Data we collect

  • Google authorization: the authorization token Google issues when you allow n8n-autopost to upload videos. We request only the youtube.upload permission.
  • Content you provide: the videos, titles and descriptions you schedule, and the upload time you choose.
  • Upload results: the video ID and upload status YouTube returns, so we can show whether an upload succeeded.

We do not read, store or share your channel data, comments, subscribers or analytics.

How we use the data

We use the data only to upload the videos you scheduled, at the time you chose, to the channel you authorized. We do not use it for advertising, profiling or any other purpose.

Where the data is stored

Authorization tokens and scheduling records are stored in private, access-controlled storage used only to run the service. Video files are stored in private cloud storage until they are uploaded.

Sharing

We do not sell or rent your data. Your videos are sent only to YouTube, through the official YouTube Data API. We share data with service providers only as far as needed to store files and run the scheduling system.

Retention

  • Authorization tokens are kept until you revoke access or ask us to delete them.
  • Video files are deleted within 30 days after they are uploaded.
  • Scheduling records are kept for up to 12 months, then deleted.

Revoking access and deleting your data

You can revoke n8n-autopost's access at any time on the Google security settings page. Scheduled uploads stop as soon as access is revoked.

To have all your stored data deleted, email blueui.design@gmail.com with the subject "Delete my data". We complete deletion within 30 days and confirm by email.

Children

The service is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their data.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we update the date at the top of this page.
